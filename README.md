**NewNet** is a beautifully designed, all-in-one macOS menu bar application built natively with SwiftUI. It brings together high-speed video and audio downloading, custom ringtone creation, automated music scrobbling, live network bandwidth monitoring, and a productivity-enhancing macOS utility suite—all living quietly in your menu bar.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **System Requirements:** Built exclusively for **Apple Silicon Macs** (M1, M2, M3, M4 or later) running macOS 15.0 or later.

---

## ✨ Features

### ⚡️ Media Downloader & Audio Cutter
* **High-Resolution Video Downloads:** Download videos up to 4K from YouTube, Twitter/X, Reddit, Vimeo, and hundreds of platforms with clean format selection and no duplicate options.
* **Audio Cutter (MM:SS):** Trim songs to your favorite segment using an intuitive time range selector with instant duration calculation.
* **Native iPhone Ringtones (.m4r):** Export trimmed audio directly into Apple’s native 256 kbps AAC `.m4r` format, preserving dynamic bass and crisp transients ready for your iPhone.
* **MP3 Audio:** Extract full songs or ringtones in high-quality MP3 format.
* **Animated GIFs:** Convert video moments into smooth GIFs with customizable duration presets.
* **Browser Cookie Support:** Seamlessly connect Safari or Chromium-based browsers to download age-restricted or member-only media without bot verification errors.

---

### 🛠 macOS Productivity Utilities
* **☕️ Keep Awake:** Prevent your Mac and display from sleeping indefinitely or for timed durations (`15m`, `30m`, `1h`, `2h`). Supports **clamshell mode** so your MacBook stays awake even when the display lid is closed. Automatically releases assertions when the battery drops below 10%.
* **📋 Clipboard History:** Instant, searchable clipboard manager for text, links, images, and files. Pin your favorites and filter by category with zero cloud sync.
* **🖱 Natural Mouse Scrolling:** Inverts the scroll wheel direction on external mice to feel natural, while automatically preserving native trackpad and Magic Mouse gestures.
* **✂️ Finder Cut & Paste:** Use **⌘X** and **⌘V** to move files directly in Finder just like standard desktop workflows.
* **🛑 Quit on Close:** Automatically closes background apps when their last window is closed, freeing up memory without terminating protected apps like browsers or Finder.

---

### 🎵 Music Scrobbling
* **Dual-Service Sync:** Automatically scrobbles your music listening history to both **Last.fm** and **ListenBrainz**.
* **Automatic Detection:** Seamlessly detects playing songs, artists, and artwork from **Apple Music** and **Spotify**.
* **Mini Player:** View playback progress, scrobble timers, and control playback directly from your menu bar.

---

### 📊 Network Bandwidth Monitor & Speed Test
* **Menu Bar Indicator:** Live, compact download and upload speed indicators right in your menu bar.
* **Network Graph:** Real-time visual traffic graphs and interface statistics.
* **Bandwidth Usage Attribution:** See which active apps are using your internet bandwidth.
* **Speed Test:** Built-in multi-threaded internet speed test powered by Cloudflare.

---

### 🔒 Privacy & Design
* **100% Local & Private:** Zero tracking, zero telemetry, and zero ads. Your data stays entirely on your Mac.
* **Native macOS Design:** Fluid Liquid Glass aesthetic, adaptive light and dark appearance, and Dynamic Type support.
* **Automatic Updates:** Silent, cryptographically verified updates powered by Sparkle.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Download the latest **`NewNet.dmg`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/sfdcnaveen/NewNet/releases) page.
2. Open the disk image and drag **`NewNet.app`** into your **`Applications`** folder.
3. Open NewNet from Spotlight or Launchpad.

> *Note: To enable macOS utilities (Clipboard History, Finder Cut & Paste, Quit on Close, and Mouse Inversion), grant Accessibility permission when prompted in macOS System Settings.*
